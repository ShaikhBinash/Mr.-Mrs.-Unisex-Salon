Good to Know

Questions, answered.

Do I need to book an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking by call or WhatsApp guarantees your preferred time, especially on weekends and festive days.

Do you serve both men and women?

Yes. We are a full unisex salon, with services for men, women and children.

Do you offer bridal packages and trials?

We do. Share your date and look ideas, and we will suggest a package and schedule a trial before the big day.

Where are you located?

Green Park, Bungalow No. 2, near Prasanna Sweets on Casa Reo Road, Nilje, Dombivli East.

Can I bring my family for back-to-back slots?

Absolutely. Tell us how many people are coming and we will line up stylists so everyone is done together.
